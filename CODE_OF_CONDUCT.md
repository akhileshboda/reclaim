# Code of Conduct

Reclaim adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct, for everyone taking part in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the code, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't welcome here.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**). It's the one private channel this project has, and conduct reports are welcome there too. You can also use GitHub's own **Report content** option on any comment.

Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block people who break this code, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
